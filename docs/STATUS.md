Audio: original 8-bit style music (jungle, boss, desert, victory jingle), 12 sound effects and rain/wind ambience, generated by `tools/audio/make_audio.py`. Checked for levels, clipping and looping; mixing judged by ear is still to do.

Enemy sprites are single poses, made to walk by the cut-out rig (legs swing,
body bobs, torso leans toward the aim and kicks when firing). The shield
trooper and heavy gunner use a stride bob instead.

## Not verified

- **Feel and balance by a human player.** The tests drive the game by script
  (teleporting and injecting hits). Enemy density, damage, boss health and
  jump distances need a play session to tune.
- Gamepad input (mapped, not tried on hardware).
- Output on a real 4K TV: screenshots were checked at 2848x1602 (the largest
  window this Mac allows), where sprites and text are sharp at 1:1.
- Exported builds (layouts need `*.txt` in the export filter).

- Worlds beyond jungle and desert: arctic, industrial, caverns, futuristic
  city, naval, fortress. Each is a new `EnvironmentDef` + art.
- Additional behaviours as needed: burrowing, conveyor/moving platforms,
  destructible terrain, interactive switches, escort targets.
- Player characters beyond one (`CharacterDef` exists, no select screen).
